Background · Regularization

SIGReg — Anti-Collapse Regularization

Regularization Loss
SIGReg(Z) = 𝔼v ~ Sd−1 normality( Z·v )
Total: MSE(ẑ, z) + λ/2 · ( SIGReg(zt) + SIGReg(zt+1) )
  • Z  batch of latent embeddings
  • v  random unit vector — projection direction
  • Each 1D projection pushed toward N(0,1)
  • No frozen encoders, EMA, or stop-gradients needed
  • Batch size matters — more samples → better normality estimate

Seeing the world through latent predictions

EPISODE 004649 · 3 context frames · 4 fps · world model + decoder: epoch 10

1 · Recorded frameThe actual observation at each time.

2 · Reconstructed observationA reconstruction head decodes the real projected embedding.

3 · Predicted futureThe head decodes autoregressively predicted embeddings after the context frames.

4 · Persistence baselineRepeat the last observed frame without predicting motion.

The reconstruction head makes the learned representation visible. Decoding an observed embedding reveals what it retains; decoding a predicted embedding reveals the model’s imagined future. LeWM’s prediction objective operates in latent space; the decoder provides this pixel-space view.

This episode’s 30 future frames: mean pixel MSE 0.0164 predicted vs 0.0464 persistence (≈65% lower). Prediction error grows over the rollout.

Proposal

A Differentiable Kinematics Model could

Capability 1

A physics probe trained on sensory data, current state, and latent representations could predict future hand placements — grounding the latent space in physical reality rather than pure pixel statistics.

Capability 2

The physics probe could shape "better" latents — ones that are action-sensitive and physically meaningful, enabling faster adaptation to new robotic setups without retraining LeWM from scratch.
